Mimo

Say what you spent. Mimo keeps the budget.

Mimo is a budgeting app for Android. Tell it what you spent or scan a receipt, and it sorts your spending, watches your savings goal and warns you before you overspend.

  • Log spending by voice, receipt photo or typing
  • See where your money went, month by month
  • Keep working offline; Mimo catches up when you reconnect
