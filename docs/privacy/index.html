Mimo

Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Mimo helps you track your spending. This page explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What Mimo stores

  • Account: your email address and password, or your Google sign-in. Passwords are stored only in hashed form.
  • Profile: the name, age, income type, income, currency and savings goal you enter.
  • Budget: your expenses (amount, category, note, date, and the words you said when you logged one by voice) and your savings goals.
  • Usage: how many AI reads you made each day, used to apply the daily limit.

What Mimo does not do

Mimo has no ads and no analytics, does not sell your data, and does not connect to your bank.

Other services that handle your data

  • Supabase hosts your account and budget. Its servers are in Singapore, so your data is processed outside your country if you live elsewhere.
  • Google Gemini reads the receipt photos and spending text you send to Mimo's AI. Mimo does not keep the photos, but they are processed by Google under its terms. Please avoid scanning documents with information you would not want processed this way.
  • Your phone's speech recognition turns your voice into text. On most Android phones this is provided by Google. Mimo receives the text, not a recording.
  • Google Sign-In shares your name and email with Mimo if you choose to use it.

Stored on your phone

Mimo keeps a copy of your budget and any changes waiting to be sent, so it can work without internet. The copy is removed when you log out. Changes not yet sent stay until you next log in.

Permissions

The microphone is used only while you log an expense by voice. The camera and photos are used only when you scan a receipt.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete your account. You can do that any time in the app under Me → Delete account, or on the delete account page. It removes your account, profile, expenses, goals and usage record for good. You can view and correct your details in the app at any time.

Children

Children should use Mimo with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us data without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests can be raised on GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so please do not include personal or financial details.
